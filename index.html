Keys and controls

Playback

Space
play / pause
< >
back / forward 5 s
Enter
back to the start
⦿ Catch playhead
jump the timeline and the caption list to the playhead and follow it; scrolling either one by hand stops following

Speakers

speaker key
tag the current caption (or the whole selection). Tagged captions move to that speaker's own timeline, and a hidden speaker timeline appears the first time they're tagged
Speakers panel
click a speaker to edit its name, key or colour; the roster is remembered for next time

Timeline

drag a caption
slide it in time (snaps to frames; hold Alt for free)
Shift + drag
move it to another timeline without changing its time
drag its edges
trim the start or end
drag on empty space
box-select several captions (Ctrl adds to the selection)
click empty space / ruler
move the playhead there
double-click empty space
add a blank caption there
← →
nudge the selection 1 frame (Shift = 10)
↑ ↓
move the selection up / down a timeline
Delete
delete the selected captions
Ctrl + wheel, − / + , slider
zoom, always centred on the playhead
Show timelines
✓ shows or hides any timeline (hidden ones keep their captions, which still render), shows a hidden speaker's timeline, or adds a new one. The – on a timeline's name hides it too
⇅ Autosort
move every tagged caption onto its speaker's timeline
drag on the Audio row
select a stretch of video; ✂ Trim (or Delete) cuts it out. Trimmed stretches show hatched, the preview skips them, and the render removes them. Click ↺ on one to bring it back

Caption list

☰ handle
click to select, drag for a range, Shift-click to extend, Ctrl-click to add or remove
▶
jump to that caption
text box
what shows in the video; | is a line break (HECK|YES)
PROD NOTES
instructions for whoever renders it (effects, colours, image links); never shown
😀 GIF + ✕
insert emoji, search GIPHY and drop a GIF link into PROD NOTES, add a caption after, delete

Editing

Backspace / Shift + Backspace
undo / redo (last 10 steps)
Esc
clear the selection
